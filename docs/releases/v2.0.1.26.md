# Release v2.0.1.26

Application commit: `cf25856` (plus `f28f2b2`). Deployed on 2026-10-28 from
18:19:05 to 18:19:14 UTC, after waiting for the one game in progress to
finish.

## Changes

- **Queue drops players who leave.** A player who joined the queue and then
  went to another lobby page (logo, Home, back) stayed queued and was later
  seated in a game they never saw, with bots playing their seat. A live
  navigation within one live_session reuses the channel topic, and Phoenix
  kills the old LiveView without `terminate/2`. This affected 10 of 1,395
  games since Sep 24, 5 of them among the 21 games with two or more humans.
  `QueueStarter` and `PrivateQueueManager` now monitor the process that adds
  each player (`QueueHolders`) and drop the player once every holding process
  has left or exited.
- **Logging for player trouble:** queue joins, leaves and drops; one line per
  game start and end; a warning when a human seat has not connected 60s
  after the start; every Google and password sign-in. `Analytics.Health`
  summarizes games, crashes, no-show seats, queue departures, sign-ups and
  sign-ins (see "Checking that players are fine" in
  `deploy/contabo/README.md`), and the pruner logs it daily.
- `GameLive` returns to the lobby with full redirects, removing about 25
  "redirecting across live_sessions" warnings a day.
- Sign-in and account pages: one 400px form width, Google's standard light
  button and logo, password field colours fixed.
- `bandit` 1.13.1 → 1.14.1 and `hackney` 4.8.6 → 4.9.1 for new advisories
  (bandit: 1-byte chunked request bodies amplifying memory use).
