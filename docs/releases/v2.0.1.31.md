19:24:32 UTC with no games in progress.

## Changes

- **Bots keep the ace of hearts.** The bot's discard kept cards whose suit
  matched trump, plus kings, so it threw away the ace of hearts (always the
  third-highest trump) whenever trump was not hearts. It now uses
  `Card.trump?/2`. In simulation (the C++ port of the bot in the
  Frank-45s-Opus research repo, 200,000 duplicate deals) the fixed bot gains
  1.34 points per hand over the old one. First tests for
  `BotPlayer.pick_discard/2`.
